		Pokémon Misery Dungeon: Explorers of the Console CHANGELOG
===============================================================================
			   0.2.0 "The Learning Update"  - October 26, 2026
===============================================================================
Save files and highscores from 0.1.x will not be compatible.
Expect scores to be significantly inflated compared to 0.1.x!
-----------------------------------ADDITIONS-----------------------------------
- 36 new Pokémon species have been added to the game, representing regional
  variants of Generation 1 Pokémon!
- 4 new starter Pokémon to choose from; Alolan Vulpix, Alolan Meowth, Galarian
  Meowth and Hisuian Growlithe. Yes, you can start as the ice fox now. Kon!
- TMs have been added to the game! There are 58 TMs in total, from generation 1
  as well as the three PMD-exclusive TMs.
- Many ranged moves (and some non-ranged moves) now play animations!
- 31 new moves for the new Pokémon, as well as for moves that are (currently)
  TM-exclusive.
- Weather has been completely overhauled and can now spawn naturally on floors.
  This becomes more common the further into the dungeon you get. There are also
  9 new weather types:
	- Cloudy: Normal-type moves are boosted in power by 33%. 
    Visibility is reduced by one tile in corridors.
	- Fog: Lowers move accuracy by 20%, but all Pokémon have 33% increased Speed. 
	Visibility is reduced by two tiles in corridoors (but not below one tile).
	- Snow: Ice-type moves are boosted by 50%.
	- Blizzard: Ice-type moves are boosted by 50% and Ice-type Pokémon have 
	increased movement speed and +50% Speed.
	- Thunderstorm: Water-type and Electric-type moves are boosted by 50%. 
	Fire-type moves are weakened by 50%.
	- And 4 more super rare weather types... I won't spoil them here ;)
- Additionally, most weather effects now play particle animations on the map.
- Floors may have lowered visibility now, and this becomes increasingly common
  as you reach higher floors.
- Dungeon floors are now timed. Linger too long and the floor will collapse,
  ending your game.
- Pokémon now have Natures! Natures work just like the main series Pokémon
  games and every Pokémon you encounter has a randomly determined Nature.
- You can now reroll your starter's IVs and Nature before starting a new game. 
  You can "lock" up to 2 of them to prevent them from being re-randomized while
  rerolling the rest. Use this if you get a good IV/Nature and want to keep it.
- You can now sort your inventory by pressing [Z] in the inventory screen. You
  can sort items by name, type, or rarity, and the game will keep sorting your
  items for you!
- You can now rename teammates from their Summary screen.
- A new "Auto Leader Switch" mode has been added, which can be toggled on and 
  off by pressing [;]. This control mode automatically switches the leader to 
  other teammates when it is their turn to move, allowing for full manual 
  control of your team in tight situations!
- The end screen now includes a copy of the entire floor's map when your game
  ended (and is also included in the text dump).
- A Debug Mode has been added for those filthy cheaters ;) (or you know, 
  debuggers) Pass the --debug parameter to the game when starting it to 
  activate it. Debug mode gives you access to a bunch of debugging and cheating
  features, but will disable high scores. Note that messing with debug features
  may lead to unexpected bugs, so try it at your own risk!
- 16 new menu screen tips.
------------------------------------CHANGES------------------------------------
- EXP has been rebalanced. The base EXP from an enemy is now multiplied by the
  number of party members in your team. Additionally, the team member that
  deals the finishing blow gets a +25% EXP bonus, and Pokémon that did not
  damage an enemy at all get a -25% EXP penalty. This should make training a
  full party much easier.
- Dungeon floor size now increases as you progress, the early floors should be
  faster now.
- Similarly, the number of items per floor and the rate of Pokémon spawns now
  increases slowly with the floor number. Expect slightly fewer items on
  earlier floors, and slightly more on later floors.
- To compensate for EXP being more abundant, base EXP from enemies has been
  reduced by 33%.
- Some items now only appear on certain floors.
	- All evolution stones now only appear on 21F and higher.
	- Sitrus Berries, Razz Berries, Nanab Berries and Pinap Berries only appear 
	  on 31F and higher.
	- Type-resistance berries only appear on 21F and higher.
	- Vitamins (except PP Up) only appear on 11F and higher.
	- PP Max only appears on 41F and higher.
	- Geo Pebbles stop appearing after 10F.
	- Gravelerocks stop appearing after 20F.
	- Sticks only appear on 1F-20F.
	- Iron Thorns only appear on 11F-30F.
	- Silver Spikes only appear on 21F-40F.
	- Corsola Twigs and Cacnea Spikes only appear on 31F and higher.
	- Gold Fangs only appear on 41F and higher.
	- Rainbow Apricorns only appear on 31F and higher.
	- Grimy Food only appears on 11F and higher.
	- Doom Seeds don't appear on 1F anymore (because they would be redundant
	  when every Pokémon is level 1 anyway)
- Lots of improvements to damage pop-ups.
	- Damage pop-ups are properly centered now.
	- Damage pop-ups have inverted colors on a critical hit.
	- New pop-ups appear when a stat is raised or lowered.
	- Some status effects also trigger a pop-up when applied.
- Now that Dark- and Steel-type Pokémon are in the game, the Black & Silver
  Apricorns are now much more common, going from Legendary rarity to Rare/Very
  Rare respectively.
- Certain "dangerous" moves (such as Explosion and Memento) are now disabled by
  default for teammates. Hopefully that means your teammates don't randomly kill
  themselves now... as much :P
- Save files are now compressed with zlib and have had some unnecessary data
  removed. This should reduce the size of save files by over 80%. I don't know
  if this will break compatability with some platforms, so let me know!
- All weather orbs are now one tier rarer.
- Fury Cutter's power has been nerfed (15 -> 10)
- Lowered the power ceiling of Reversal and Flail (250 -> 200)
- Abra doesn't start with Confusion anymore. Not really necessary now that it
  can learn moves by TM.
- The terminal is now cleared on screen refresh.
- The game now returns to the title screen instead of exiting when saving and
  quitting or after the game ends.
-------------------------------------FIXES-------------------------------------
- Diglett now evolves at level 26 instead of 24 (whoops)
- Lowered the luminous floor vision radius from 100 to 56 to partially fix
  performance issues caused by excessive lighting calculations
- Fixed Luminous Orb's effect being permanent
- Allies won't use Struggle anymore if all of their moves are manually
  disabled.
- Apricorns are no longer affected by Pierce Throw (which was causing them not
  to work)
- Fixed HP values below -99 causing graphical glitches in the interface.
- Fixed some moves having the wrong type.
	- Nasty Plot is now properly Dark-type (and not Fairy)
	- Counter is now properly Fighting-type (and not Dark)
- Totter Seeds actually work now when thrown.
- Fixed the screen not refreshing properly when a move needs to be replaced on
  evolving a Pokémon.
- Fixed another double message, this time when using a Power Toss Orb.
- Fixed a typo with the Transform Orb's description.
- Removed some internal unused code. I have no idea if this actually improves
  performance/mem usage or not, but I doubt it will make a difference anyway
  lol
- Miscellaneous JSON fixes.
